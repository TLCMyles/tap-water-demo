Tap Water — a living-simulation demo

Six Blocks

Los Angeles County is sealed. The water that runs from every tap changed some people and took the old world from all of them. You are Tales — thirty-six, unremarkable, a former security guard — and, since the outbreak, able to become almost anyone you touch.

You've come to one small neighborhood. Six streets, two hundred and fifty people, all of them living lives that started long before you arrived.

No one is waiting for you. If something matters here, you'll have to notice it — by watching, by talking, by paying attention. What you do, and who you choose to be, is the whole game.
