Tap Water — Vertical Slice

Six Blocks

Los Angeles County is sealed. The water that runs from every tap changed some people and took the old world from all of them. You are Tales — thirty-six, unremarkable, a former security guard — and, since the outbreak, able to become almost anyone you touch.

You've come to one small neighborhood. Six streets. Two hundred and fifty people who were living their lives long before you arrived, and will keep living them whether or not you do anything at all.

There are no quest markers here. No one is waiting for you. If something matters on these blocks, you'll have to notice it — by watching, by talking, by paying attention. What you do (and who you choose to be) is the whole game.

A living-simulation demo. Everything you're about to see — the shortages, the rumors, the people's choices, the story that results — emerges from systems, not a script. Play it twice and it won't go the same way.
